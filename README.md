Hardcore Combat Overhaul (H-COM) is a massive gameplay overhaul and expansion built on top of Second Wave and Section 17, reworking nearly every aspect of Planetary Annihilation: TITANS and pushing the poor PA engine to its limits to deliver a far more immersive battlefield experience.

It expands upon the foundations of both mods, introducing new mechanics, technologies, units and gameplay systems while reworking existing ones. Underused mechanics—such as armor types, target priorities and exemptions, radar stealth, upgradable turrets and units, Tier 3 technology, transport capacities and interceptable projectiles—are brought into the core of gameplay.

All units have been reworked to resemble real-world weapon systems, and dozens of new ones have been added: from C-RAM turrets and anti-matter railguns to AWACS aircraft. Everything you'll ever need to build your dream blob/base.

Economy, production, vision, detection and combat have all received substantial changes. Balance follows logic, unless it doesn't...

Anyways. Forget what you know about PA and experiment to discover all the exploits!

DISCLAIMER: H-COM is a standalone server-side gameplay overhaul. Second Wave and Section 17 are required dependencies. Due to extensive changes to core gameplay systems, H-COM is incompatible with other server-side gameplay mods and expansions. Do not enable other server mods alongside it unless explicitly supported.

DISCLAIMER NO. 2: AI armies are not supported. H-COM is designed for PvP.
