This modification expands and improves nearly every aspect of the game, compiling assets from some of the greatest PA mods out there and pushing the poor PA engine to its limits to deliver a far more immersive battlefield experience. It brings underused mechanics—such as armor types, target priorities and exemptions, radar stealth, upgradable turrets and units, Tier 3 tech, transport sizes, and interceptable projectiles—into the core of gameplay. All units have been reworked to resemble real-world weapon systems, and many new ones have been added: from C-RAM turrets and anti-matter railguns to AWACS aircraft—giving you everything you'll ever need to build your dream blob/base. Balance follows logic, unless it doesn't... Anyways. Forget what you know about PA and experiment to find all the exploits! DISCLAIMER: This is a standalone mod and is incompatible with other expansions. Please disable all server mods to fully enjoy it.
